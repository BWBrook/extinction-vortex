**Caption.** W denotes the Williams ID. Windows show the deposited counts.
The primary analysis stops at the first count at or below one: in 2002 for
the crane, and at the terminal zero for the other eight. Source matching does
not establish complete census coverage, and it does not reproduce the
historical estimator. "Unrecovered" describes this audit's evidence boundary,
not absence.

**Dependence.** Individual observations and age or breeding composition can
add information beyond annual totals, but they share animals, years and
observation errors with the counts. Shared monitoring does not justify
treating their likelihoods as independent. Rates inferred only from the same
decline are not independent prior evidence.

**Full provenance and conflicts:** the source audits of 8 and 13 October, the
Haley conflict register, and the detailed ledger
(`nine_series_evidence_ledger_20261014.md`).
