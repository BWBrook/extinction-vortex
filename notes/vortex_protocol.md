## 1. Scientific aims and minimum contribution — Fable formal draft 0.2

This section supersedes Astra's planning summary. Astra's requirements are
retained; where they are now quantified, the subsection is named.

**Q1 (primary, empirical).** Take the nine-population Fagan–Holmes (F&H)
terrestrial sample. Are the observed values of the five published
terminal-trajectory statistics T1–T5 (§2.1) compatible with their distribution
under a population-matched no-feedback process M0 (§3.1)? The simulated data
must pass through the same selection, census processing and estimator as the
observed data.

**Q2 (primary, simulation).** Under the same matched design, how often does the
Part 1 decision rule (§4.3) (a) flag a departure and (b) attribute it to
feedback? This is measured for data generated by:
- feedback in expected growth (M_A, M_G);
- external deterioration (M_D);
- observation change (M_O).

**Q3 (secondary, empirical).** Q1 repeated for the accessible Williams series
under the published Williams pipeline, stratified by census type. Nine
populations overlap, so this is not an independent replication.

**Q4 (conditional extension).** Only if gate D (§4.4) passes: the abundance
dependence of expected per-capita growth (β, §2.3) in the frozen GPDD census
frame.

**Minimum contribution:** Q1 together with Q2. Q4 is not needed to give the
study an empirical component.

**Part 1 outcome rules.** These are fixed before any new test.

| Outcome | Condition |
|---|---|
| **O1** compatible with the specified null | p_joint ≥ 0.05, *and* the joint rule has power ≥ 0.80 against the minimum meaningful effect δ_min (§3.3) |
| **O3** insufficient precision or identification | p_joint ≥ 0.05 with that power < 0.80, *or* the §4.3 validation fails |
| **O2** departure needing explanation | p_joint < 0.05 |

An O2 departure is called *consistent with feedback in expected growth* only if
the Q2 classification rule meets both criteria below. Otherwise it is reported
as *departure, cause unresolved*.
- Feedback is correctly attributed with probability ≥ 0.80 under M_A or M_G at
  δ_min.
- Misattribution is ≤ 0.10 under every M_D and M_O scenario.

No outcome identifies genetic rather than other biological mechanisms. M_G is
distinguished from M_A only as far as the Q2 matrix shows it can be. A large
residual is a result, not a reason to abandon the study. Failure of gate D says
nothing about whether feedback is absent.

## 2. Estimands and comparison populations — Fable formal draft 0.2

### 2.1 Part 1 design and statistics

For each population i, the design records:
- the first census N_i,first and its year;
- the census years, including documented interpolations (F&H report one
  interpolated census each for the wild dog and the middle spotted
  woodpecker);
- the extinction rule E (F&H: first census with N ≤ 1; Williams: first zero);
- the extinction time L_i, in years from the first census to the extinction
  census;
- the published final-step convention, N_0 := 1.

Time to extinction is s = (year of extinction census) − (census year).

The statistics are those F&H published, computed by the Stage B estimator:

| Statistic | Definition | Published value (F&H) |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | slope of D_s = ln(N_s/N_{s+1}) on s, all transitions; multilevel regression with AR(1) residuals | +0.034 (intercept −0.50) |
| T2 | T1 excluding the final transition | +0.011 (−0.22) |
| T3 | slope on s of the squared T1 residuals | −0.047 (0.66) |
| T4 | T3 excluding the final transition | −0.012 (0.22) |
| T5 | period coefficient (s ≤ 9 vs s ≥ 10) in the regression of s on ln N_s, the "value of *n* individuals" | to be transcribed exactly at Stage B |

**Estimands.**
- p_k: the two-sided predictive tail probability of T_k^obs under the
  calibrated M0, for k = 1, …, 5.
- p_joint = P(D^rep ≥ D^obs), where D is the Mahalanobis distance of
  (T1, …, T5) from the null predictive mean under the null predictive
  covariance.
- For Q2: the matrix Π[g, c] = P(decision c | generator g, matched design),
  with c ∈ {O1, O2-feedback, O2-unresolved, O3}.

### 2.2 Williams series (secondary)

- **Published statistics.** The Williams statistics as published: the slope of
  R_t on years to extinction, residual variability, and the body-size
  interaction. They are computed with the authors' archived code on the 31
  public series.
- **Raw-count check.** An F&H-type D_s analysis on raw counts for the 13
  complete-count series.
- **Unavailable series.** The four unavailable series are reported as missing
  and never imputed.
- **Processing questions for Stage B.** The open questions from T3 (min–max
  rescaling before the state-space fit, R_t on the rescaled state, the
  terminal-zero rule) are verified at Stage B before any claim is made about
  Williams et al.

### 2.3 Part 2 estimand (conditional extension)

- **Frame.** Every GPDD census series in one pinned release that meets the
  structural eligibility rules of §5.3, entrants and non-entrants alike.
- **Primary unit strata.**
  - Individuals, with N* = 50.
  - Breeding pairs or territories, with N* = 25.

  N* follows IUCN Criterion D for Critically Endangered (fewer than 50 mature
  individuals). It is fixed before any power calculation and never tuned.
  Other units are excluded from the primary analysis.
- **Process model.**
  - E[N_{t+1} | N_t = n] = n · λ_i(t, n) · g(n; β).
  - log λ_i(t, n) = a_i + d_i (t − t̄_i) + γ_i (ln n − m_i): a
    population-specific level, a linear trend, and Gompertz compensation.
  - g(n; β) = exp(−β (1/n − 1/N*)) for 0 < n < N*, and g = 1 for n ≥ N*.
  - β > 0 means feedback (lower expected per-capita growth at small n);
    β < 0 means compensation. Report β and Δ10 = 1 − g(10; β).
- **Zeros and recolonisation.**
  - Per-capita growth is defined only at latent n > 0.
  - Transitions into zero, including direct moves from above N* to zero, enter
    the likelihood as process outcomes.
  - A recolonisation after an observed zero is modelled by an immigration term
    ι_i ≥ 0, with a frame-level prior, or as non-detection in the observation
    model.
- **Ascertainment.**
  - The primary likelihood is a risk-set likelihood over the whole frame.
    Entry into the small state therefore defines reporting strata, not who is
    analysed, and no outcome-based inclusion enters the primary model.
  - Initial conditions: condition on each series' first observation, with
    random-effect means depending on initial log abundance (Wooldridge 2005,
    doi:10.1002/jae.770).
  - The frame is still a curated database. The target is that monitored
    sampling frame, not all wild populations.
- **Secondary analyses.**
  - An entrant-only likelihood conditioned on the full pre-entry history.
  - The alternative feedback form g(n) = n/(n + A).

## 3. Generative models and calibration — Fable formal draft 0.2

### 3.1 Null M0: abundance-independent expected per-capita growth

- **Transition.** N_{t+1} = S_{t+1} + R_{t+1}, where:
  - survivors S_{t+1} ~ Binomial(N_t, s_{i,t});
  - recruits R_{t+1} ~ Poisson(N_t · f_{i,t}).
- **Environmental variation.**
  - It acts on survival (logit-normal) and on recruitment (lognormal), with a
    common scale σ_i.
  - Both are centred numerically so that E[s_{i,t}] + E[f_{i,t}] = λ_i = s_i +
    f_i, whatever the abundance.
- **Adult survival s_i.**
  - Taken from published species sources and fixed in a table before the
    validation freeze.
  - With a binomial survival component, demographic variance is
    s_i(1 − s_i) + f_i per individual. Long-lived species are therefore not
    given Poisson-sized demographic noise, which would make the null too noisy
    and favour O1.
  - A Poisson-only demographic model is a sensitivity analysis.

### 3.2 Alternatives

| Model | Specification | Parameter values |
|---|---|---|
| M_A | expected growth multiplied by g(n; β) (§2.3) | Δ10 ∈ {δ_min, 2·δ_min} |
| M_G | expected growth multiplied by exp(−B·F_t), with F_{t+1} = F_t + (1 − F_t)/(2·r·N_t) and F_0 = 0 at the first census | B ∈ {6.15, 12.3} diploid lethal equivalents (12.3 from O'Grady et al. 2006, doi:10.1016/j.biocon.2006.05.016); r = N_e/N ∈ {0.1, 0.3} (Frankham 1995, doi:10.1017/s0016672300034455) |
| M_D | λ_i(t) = λ_{i,0} · h(t) | h ∈ {exp(d·t), linear on the log scale; exp(d·t²/L_i), accelerating; a single drop by factor k at a uniformly random time}. d and k are calibrated with the other nuisances. |
| M_O | census detection that falls at low abundance | p(n) = 1 − 0.3·exp(−n/10); binomial census error with p = 0.9 for series labelled complete counts |

Every generator replicates the documented interpolations, missing years, the
endpoint rule E and the final-step convention.

### 3.3 Minimum meaningful effect δ_min

δ_min is the smallest Δ10 for which M_A raises the 10-year extinction
probability from N_0 = 20 by at least 0.10 (absolute) over M0. It is evaluated
at the median calibrated M0 parameters of the nine populations.
- **Computation.** 10^5 runs at each grid value of Δ10, in steps of 0.025.
  This is done once, at Stage C, before the validation runs, and reported
  alongside the M0 baseline.
- **Fixed in advance.** The rule and the computation are fixed now. Only the
  calibrated parameters are inputs, and these come from an outcome-exposed
  sample (§6).
- **Large values.** If δ_min > 0.5, report that only large feedback is
  decision-relevant at these parameters.

### 3.4 Calibration

**Priors** (per population):

| Parameter | Prior |
|---|---|
| log λ_i | U(log 0.4, log 1.3) |
| σ_i | U(0.02, 1.0) |
| s_i | U(s_lit − 0.05, s_lit + 0.05) |
| ρ_i (AR(1)) | fixed at 0 in the primary analysis; U(0, 0.7) as a sensitivity |
| generator parameters | as in §3.2 |

**Selection.**
- Primary: the exact matched design. Each run starts at N_i,first, follows the
  observed census schedule, and first meets rule E at L_i, with no earlier
  extinction.
- Sensitivity: the original studies' inclusion windows.
  - F&H: monitored at least 12 years to N ≤ 1.
  - Williams: at least 10 years; at least 5 observations before the zero; a
    terminal zero; no interior zero.

**Calibration features C_i.** These are never the tested statistics:
- the mean squared one-step log change, excluding the final transition;
- the lag-1 autocorrelation of one-step log changes, excluding the final
  transition;
- the fraction of increasing transitions.

**ABC procedure.**
1. Draw 10^5 parameter sets from the prior per population and generator.
2. Keep the runs that satisfy the selection rule.
3. Retain the 2,000 nearest by standardised Euclidean distance on C_i, or all
   of them if fewer survive. Report acceptance counts.
4. Build replicate datasets by drawing one accepted (parameters, trajectory)
   pair per population and applying the full Stage B estimator: R = 10,000 for
   the empirical test, R = 2,000 within validation.

**Why this answers the calibration objections.**
- Drift, σ and demographic variance are calibrated jointly, under the same
  selection as the data. Nothing relies on Brownian drift invariance or on
  unselected pre-terminal estimates, so the earlier claim that "drift barely
  matters" is withdrawn.
- Whether the resulting partial predictive p-values (Bayarri & Berger 2000,
  doi:10.2307/2669749) are properly calibrated is not assumed. It is tested in
  §4.3 by simulation-based calibration (Talts et al. 2018, arXiv:1804.06788).

**Misspecification checks.**
- A posterior predictive check of C_i.
- Calibrated σ_i compared with independent estimates from non-extinct GPDD
  series of the same species or genus, where they exist. This is a
  plausibility check only.
- Demographic-variance model: binomial-survival vs Poisson.
- AR(1) on vs off.
- Endpoint rule E: ≤ 1 vs 0.

**Monte Carlo stability.** Repeat the empirical p_joint with a doubled
acceptance pool and a new seed, and report both. A difference greater than 0.01
triggers a larger pool.

## 4. Stages, outputs and decision rules — Fable formal draft 0.2

| Stage | Concrete output | Condition for proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| A. Protocol and metadata | Search/eligibility rules, audited source map, exposed-data record, units and observation schedules | Resolve the specifications required below; preserve outcome exposure honestly |
| B. Published-data reconstruction | Original-versus-reconstructed statistics and a discrepancy table, with scripts and input hashes | Criterion in §4.2. Do not silently alter dates, endpoints or observations to obtain agreement. |
| C. Part 1 calibrated comparison | Observed statistics against population-matched null distributions, plus false-attribution and power results across candidate processes | §4.3 validation passes before the empirical p-values are computed; report all three outcomes from §1 |
| D. Part 2 identification gate | Synthetic recovery at the audited frame structure, including censored and immediate-zero entrants | §4.4 criteria; otherwise stop the empirical extension |
| E. Forward analysis, conditional | The prespecified estimate, uncertainty and sensitivity results for the frozen frame | Open additional post-entry responses only after the gate and the analysis freeze |

### 4.1 Order

Fable accepts Astra's ordering. The Stage A metadata audit, including the
structure of the Part 2 frame, comes before the decisive gate. Illustrative
power runs may come earlier, but they are labelled illustrative and never
serve as the gate.

### 4.2 Stage B criterion

- **Target.** Reproduce the published T1–T4 within ±0.005, and T5 within ±0.05
  of its published value (or within the published rounding). Use the archived
  inputs and the documented processing.
- **Discrepancies to reconcile first.** The archived reconciliation
  ([fh_table1_reconciliation.txt](t3/fh_table1_reconciliation.txt)) found 24
  of 27 Table 1 summaries reproducing from the Williams series. The exceptions
  are:
  - whooping crane, final count 2 vs 1 (the ≤ 1 vs 0 endpoint rule);
  - wood turtle B, increase 53 vs 44;
  - Hawaiian crow, where the transcription of the "never increased" footnote
    is ambiguous.

  It also found 150 non-zero censuses, against F&H's 148.
- **If the criterion is not met.** After the windows and endpoints are
  reconciled, define a labelled *reconstructed estimator* explicitly, and carry
  both the published and the reconstructed values. The Part 1 test applies the
  reconstructed estimator identically to the observed and the simulated data.

### 4.3 Stage C validation

This runs once, on frozen code, before the empirical p-values are computed.

- **Calibration under M0.**
  - Draw 200 synthetic nine-population datasets from M0, at parameters sampled
    from the calibrated posterior. Pass each through the full ABC and test
    pipeline with R = 2,000.
  - Pass if p_joint < 0.05 in 4 to 16 of the 200 datasets (the central 95%
    binomial range at a nominal rate of 0.05) *and* a Kolmogorov–Smirnov test
    of p_joint uniformity gives p > 0.01.
- **Power and attribution.**
  - Run 200 datasets per scenario:
    - M_A at δ_min and at 2·δ_min;
    - M_G at (B, r) ∈ {(12.3, 0.1), (6.15, 0.3)};
    - M_D in each of its three forms;
    - M_O in both forms.
  - Report the Π matrix with Monte Carlo standard errors.
- **Feedback classification.** ABC model choice among {M0, M_A, M_G, M_D, M_O}
  on the C and T features. A departure is attributed to feedback if
  P(M_A ∪ M_G | data) ≥ 0.8. The rule's operating characteristics come from the
  same runs and enter the outcome rules in §1.
- **Failed fits.**
  - Refit with an alternative optimiser and new starting values.
  - Record persistent failures. If they exceed 2% in any scenario, revise the
    estimator as a documented deviation before the empirical test.
  - Report p-value bounds that treat failures as most and least extreme.
- **Development vs final validation.**
  - Tuning uses seeds 1–999 and listed development scenarios. This covers ABC
    distance weights, tolerance and optimiser settings.
  - The final validation uses seeds ≥ 10^6 on frozen code.
  - This guards against tuning the procedure to particular draws. It does not
    test misspecification beyond the listed generators.

### 4.4 Stage D gate (Part 2, after the Stage A frame audit)

- **Runs.** 500 synthetic frames per scenario, matching the audited structure:
  series lengths, gaps, unit strata and entry counts.
- **Scenarios.** A 16-run fractional factorial plus 4 corner runs, over:
  - heterogeneity in a_i and d_i: low or high;
  - σ: 0.1 or 0.3;
  - deterioration: none, linear, accelerating, or a step;
  - census error: exact counts, or binomial with p = 0.9;
  - informative censoring: none, or 20% of entrants stopping after extinction
    or recovery.
- **Pass criteria.** All must hold:
  - type I error of a one-sided 5% test of β > 0 is ≤ 0.07 under every
    no-feedback scenario;
  - power at δ_min is ≥ 0.80 in the median scenario and ≥ 0.70 in every
    scenario;
  - misattribution (β > 0 declared under deterioration without feedback) is
    ≤ 0.10;
  - 90% intervals for β cover the true value 85–95% of the time;
  - fit failures are ≤ 5%.
- **On failure.** If any criterion fails, Part 2 reports *insufficient
  identification* and stops. Nothing is retuned against forward responses.

### 4.5 Compute

Stage C validation is about 200 datasets × 10 scenarios × (9 × 10^5 simulated
trajectories plus 2,000 mixed-model fits). That is roughly 4 × 10^6 fits, or
about 220 core-hours at 0.2 s per fit. It therefore runs on `deep-server` after
a timed benchmark on `wsl-laptop`, following the compute instructions in
AGENTS.md. Stage D is of similar order.

## 5. Data compilation and source appraisal — Astra draft

### 5.1 Two distinct evidence sets

**Published reanalysis set.** Start with the named Fagan–Holmes populations and
the Williams source mapping, retaining both study identifiers and a stable
population identifier. This is a closed case reconstruction, not a systematic
sample of extinctions. The [T3 inventory](t3/vortex_series_inventory.csv) is a
lead ledger. Its 36 rows, 13 complete-count labels and 10 supported-extirpation
labels are provisional classifications; the report explicitly says primary
extirpation records were not checked. The nine-series overlap is recorded, but
window/endpoint discrepancies and the reported 150-versus-148 nonzero censuses
remain unresolved. The archived [Table 1 reconciliation](t3/fh_table1_reconciliation.txt)
matches 24 of 27 scalar fields, not the fitted statistics; its Williams spans
end at the last nonzero census. Replay requires the manifest-matching source
CSV and verification of its hash. Existing CSVs do not demonstrate exact
reproduction of the fitted statistics.

The [Williams Dryad deposit](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.jwstqjq8c) is public;
Astra checked the repository API's CC0 licence declaration on 5 October 2026
(version 2, version ID 111752). The eventual source manifest must retain the
downloaded files, dates, versions and hashes. The current 31-public/4-confidential
mapping needs verification against source files. Unavailable raw series remain
explicitly unavailable; do not substitute invented values to reconstruct 35.

**Forward sampling frame.** Enumerate the GPDD census metadata from one fixed
version first, including nonentrants. The primary discovery route must not
select populations because they subsequently declined, recovered or went extinct.
Exclude supplementary bibliographic discoveries and any additional database
from the primary GPDD estimate; retain them as labelled secondary ascertainment
strata. This fixes our discovery rule without assuming the database itself is
free of monitoring or reporting bias. Full LPD access is optional and outside the
public-data core; accepting its agreement or contacting providers requires
Barry's decision. Verify the licence/version of any open LPD subset separately.

### 5.2 Search specification

Freeze the date cutoff, database versions and translated executable queries
before the planned compilation. Cover records available through that cutoff,
without a lower publication-year limit. Search OpenAlex and Crossref, named data
repositories and one backward/forward citation pass from the seed studies and
eligible compilations. A second citation pass is justified only for newly found
compilations and must be logged. Record inaccessible records rather than
silently excluding them. No language exclusion is proposed; translation or
access limits must be reported. No author contact is assumed authorized.

Use separate search families:

| Purpose | Draft query concepts / seed |
|---|---|
| Competing explanations and prior work | `"extinction vortex"`; `"extinction" AND ("conditioning" OR "selection bias" OR "first passage" OR "null model")`; exact titles and citation links for Fagan–Holmes, Williams, Boettiger–Hastings, Dennis, Nabutanyi–Wittmann, Hefley, and both Griffen/Drake 2009 passage-time/final-decline papers |
| Outcome-neutral discovery of monitoring data | `("population" OR "abundance") AND ("census" OR "monitoring") AND ("time series" OR "long term" OR "annual counts")`; GPDD source citations and references in eligible monitoring compilations |

These are logical query templates, not claims that both APIs accept identical
Boolean syntax. Archive each provider's actual query, filters, retrieval date,
pagination/completeness status and returned identifiers. Verify translation
against known seed records before freezing the executable search. Neither a
search-engine ranking nor a citation count establishes search completeness.

The [5 October orientation-search record](vortex_prior_work_search_20261005.md)
provides executed query URLs and retrieved identifiers, with explicit coverage
limits. These queries inform the search specification; they are not a completed
systematic compilation or a retroactive search freeze.

Deduplicate publications by DOI/identifier and then metadata; deduplicate
datasets by population, spatial boundary, census definition, period and source
lineage. Retain overlapping versions as linked records. Do not count a reused
population as independent evidence. Report separate flows for publications,
datasets/populations and threshold entrants, since these are different units.

### 5.3 Eligibility and screening

Both agents independently record include/exclude/uncertain decisions and source
locations before opening the other's decisions. Preserve their initial decisions,
resolve discrepancies against the source, and retain unresolved cases as uncertain.
Report agreement and disagreement counts; a confidence score or model agreement
does not establish eligibility. The existing T3 inventory has already been seen
and cannot be retrospectively labelled independently screened.

For the forward primary frame, include entrants and nonentrants satisfying:

- a defined wild population or local population with a sufficiently stable area
  and census target, and a source-supported observation protocol;
- absolute abundance in a defined biological unit, with evidence for completeness
  or enough detection information for the specified observation model. A database
  label alone is insufficient. Keep individuals, breeding units and estimates
  separate until their model/threshold interpretation is justified;
- observation dates and units that permit the chosen transition interval, plus
  the prespecified initial-history information. Missing years remain missing; multiple
  visits and changes in survey design must be identified;

Within that frame, define threshold-entry reporting strata using history
available at entry, retaining direct entry to zero in the joint fate/abundance
analysis. An observed crossing is not an inclusion requirement. Series already
below the threshold when monitoring begins form an initial-small group with
unknown earlier entry history, not invented observed crossings.

Eligibility must not require a minimum number of later positive counts,
survival to the horizon, recovery, a verified extinction or complete follow-up.
Administrative opportunity to observe an H-year horizon can be defined from
calendar dates fixed independently of outcome. Actual early monitoring cessation
remains in the cohort with censoring and its documented or unknown reason.

Indices, densities without known sampled area/effort, inferred model outputs,
undefined population boundaries and incompatible units remain separate from the
primary count cohort. Preserve the reason for exclusion or secondary use rather
than silently converting proxies into individuals. Translocation/release studies
are a separate candidate module: manager-set release size is not automatically
exogenous, because allocation may depend on species, site quality and perceived
risk. That module needs its own assignment model and novelty assessment.

### 5.4 Extraction, provenance and bias appraisal

Use one compact population ledger, linked to immutable source files. Record:

| Field group | Minimum contents |
|---|---|
| Identity and access | Stable population/source IDs, taxon, spatial boundary, source citation/page, licence/version, file hash, duplicate/overlap links |
| Observation design | Biological unit/stage, census/estimate/index, effort/area, evidence for detection, observation dates and season, interval, missingness and design changes; source and assumptions for any unit conversion |
| Demographic inputs | Source/page for survival estimates, population and life stage measured, time interval and census timing, uncertainty, and concordance with the counted unit; generation length if used |
| Entry and monitoring | Observed history used for entry, entry date/unit, available administrative horizon, monitoring-end date and whether its reason is known |
| Provenance and endpoints | Reported interpolation/rounding/carry-forward, original versus processed values, source evidence for local extirpation, non-detection, captivity or recolonization |
| Ascertainment and covariates | Why the population was monitored/reported, measured external drivers, interventions, population dependence and source-level clustering |
| Review | Each initial eligibility decision and evidence, adjudication, reason for exclusion/secondary use, exposure status |

Appraise endpoint validity, detection, interpolation, sampling-frame selection,
informative cessation and external-driver confounding separately; do not reduce
them to a single quality score. Repeated identical plover counts alone neither
establish interpolation nor quantify variance distortion. Preserve them as
recorded while seeking their provenance. Use source-supported observation
processes and prespecified sensitivity bounds where facts remain uncertain.

The GPDD pilot's 65/59/52, 44/37/27 and 39/33/20 totals are threshold/follow-up
**observation counts**, not verified calendar-year cohorts. At threshold 50,
the reported 37 with at least five later observations include only 12 labelled
All Individuals; the rest include pairs, territories, nests and other units.
These are planning summaries, not the final eligible sample or a rationale for
choosing whichever threshold supplies most apparent power.

## 6. Exposure, freezes and deviations — Astra draft

The [exposure log](vortex_exposure_log.csv) records known access and its evidence.
Distinguish files accessed computationally, values displayed to an agent, published
outcomes already known, and fitted analyses. Include delegates: withholding their
raw output from the parent does not make their role unexposed.

Part 1 is an openly outcome-exposed reanalysis. A freeze can precede its new
tests, but cannot make its published results unknown. The GPDD pilot read real
abundance vectors to determine entry and later observation counts; an earlier
pilot also summarized terminal-zero frequencies. The open LPD subset's terminal
zero total has been reported. Individual forward trajectories may remain
unexamined by the analysts, but a blanket claim that these databases' outcomes
have never been inspected would be incorrect. Record the narrower claim and its
verification limits.

Use three small, staged records:

1. **Search/design freeze:** commit the agreed search, eligibility, structural
   extraction fields, exposure record and Part 1 reconstruction specification.
   Record unresolved specifications explicitly; a document containing unresolved
   decisive choices is not frozen for that analysis.
2. **Analysis/validation freeze:** after the metadata audit and documented pilots,
   commit estimators, parameter sources, candidate models, numerical gates,
   multiplicity rules and final simulation design before the decisive validation
   and new empirical tests. Further Part 1 adaptation remains exposed work.
3. **Forward release record:** if the gate passes without tuning to forward
