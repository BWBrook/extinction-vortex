## 1. Which no-feedback model do you approve as the primary development candidate for the reconstructed T1-T4 analysis?

Both options exclude direct abundance feedback. The iid model uses independent Gaussian environmental noise. The ar_cat model adds AR(1) environmental persistence and occasional external catastrophes; its environmental transition law excludes abundance, although environment and abundance can still be associated. The other model will remain a mandatory labelled sensitivity.

Astra and Fable recommend ar_cat: it provides a broader external-variation explanation to test before interpreting terminal signatures as feedback. It was the only implemented candidate meeting the earlier draw-budget rule near observed calibration summaries, but this does not establish that catastrophes are necessary. The model and exploratory priors follow exposure to those summaries.

The warning is possible loss of discrimination. At one development feedback strength, raw diagnostic p < .05 occurred in 29/30 iid trials and 21/30 ar_cat trials. Both the generator and reference changed, with shared random streams: the difference does not isolate a cost caused by the broader reference. Under the tested hard-distance rule, detections were only 20/30 and 14/30; calibration failures also made both null arms fail the intended gate. None of these rates is validated power.
